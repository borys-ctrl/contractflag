# How to Deploy ContractFlag — Step by Step

You need about 20 minutes. No coding required.

---

## PART 1 — Upload the code to GitHub (10 min)

1. Go to github.com and sign in

2. Click the big green "New" button (top left)

3. Fill in:
   - Repository name: contractflag
   - Keep everything else as default
   - Click "Create repository"

4. On the next page, look for the link that says "uploading an existing file"
   - Click it

5. You'll see a drag-and-drop area that says "Drag files here"
   - Open the ZIP file you downloaded from Claude
   - You'll see a folder called "contractflag" with files inside it
   - Drag ALL the files and folders into that GitHub upload area

6. Scroll down, click "Commit changes"

---

## PART 2 — Deploy to Vercel (5 min)

1. Go to vercel.com and sign in with your GitHub account

2. Click "Add New Project"

3. You'll see your "contractflag" repo listed — click "Import"

4. Click "Deploy" (don't change anything)

5. Wait 1-2 minutes — Vercel builds the app

---

## PART 3 — Add your Anthropic API key (5 min)

This is the secret key that powers the AI analysis.
Without this, the app won't work.

1. Go to console.anthropic.com
   - Sign up if you don't have an account
   - Go to "API Keys" → click "Create Key"
   - Copy the key (it starts with "sk-ant-...")
   - IMPORTANT: save it somewhere safe, you can only see it once

2. Go back to vercel.com
   - Click on your "contractflag" project
   - Click "Settings" (top menu)
   - Click "Environment Variables" (left sidebar)
   - Click "Add New"
   - Name:  ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
   - Value: paste your key here (sk-ant-...)
   - Click "Save"

3. Now redeploy so it picks up the key:
   - Click "Deployments" (top menu)
   - Click the three dots "..." next to the top deployment
   - Click "Redeploy"
   - Wait 1 minute

---

## PART 4 — Get your live link

1. Click "Domains" in your Vercel project
2. You'll see something like: contractflag.vercel.app
3. That's your live app — share that link with anyone!

---

## You're done.

Anyone who visits your link can:
- Paste or upload a contract
- Get a full risk report in 60 seconds
- Without you seeing their contract at all
